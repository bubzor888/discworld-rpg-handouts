# You Are Something Odd

- **Tip:** you'll need to work a bit harder at this one, so if you're newer to roleplaying or don't know Discworld very well, consider switching to something else.
- **What you are:** something that really shouldn't be able to think, but does. A cow, a candlestick, a sword, a swarm of bees, a book of fairytales... Enough magic leaked into you to wake up. You can form your own opinion on how you feel about that.
- **Examples from the books:** **The Luggage** (a sapient pearwood travelling trunk on hundreds of little legs, bad-tempered, treats walls as optional), **Gaspode** (a mangy talking dog), **Maurice** (a clever cat who ate a mouse that ate magical rubbish), **the Librarian** (a wizard turned orangutan), and wizards' staffs and hats that gain opinions.
- **Stereotypes:** *takes being whatever it is very seriously*. A candlestick is vain about being tall and afraid of draughts, a cat wants people to think it is just a cat, and a swarm of bees is a committee.
- **Names:** your old name (Gaspode) or your job (the Luggage, the Librarian).
