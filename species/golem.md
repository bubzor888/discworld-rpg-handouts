# You Are a Golem

- **What you are:** a figure of baked clay, made by priests who weren't great sculptors, and animated by the **chem**: a scroll of words (scripture or instructions) sealed in your head. No new golems have been made for hundreds of years, so you're probably patched and mended.
- **Body:** most golems lack the ability to talk and typically **communicate through writing**, with chalk and slate. Fun tip: write your lines on cards. You don't sleep, eat, or need shelter, and you never get tired.
- **Obedience:** while still under contract, you follow instructions *to the letter*: golems have washed dishes so thin you can see through them, and pumped a handle every eight seconds for 240 years.
- **Society:** many see you as a thing, not a person. You do the jobs even trolls won't. But times are changing: the **Golem Trust** buys golems their freedom and the free ones save to buy more. A quiet revolution.
- **Stereotypes:** *calm, formal, gentle, relentlessly literal and patient*. Answers in short slate-sized messages and never leaves a task half-done.
- **Names:** old ones are named for gods or ideas (Anghammarad, Dorlf); newer ones for their job (Pump 19) or from a kindly former owner.
