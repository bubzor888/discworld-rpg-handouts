# You Are a Werewolf

- **What you are:** a shapeshifter from Überwald (think fictional Transylvania). You're normally human and can change at will, but the **week of the full moon** forces you into wolf shape. Werewolves are mostly **born**, not made (it runs in bloodlines), though whether a bite can pass it on is disputed.
- **Forms:** as a human you have thumbs, better eyesight and the ability to think things through. As a wolf you have claws, quick combat reflexes and a nose so good you can "see" smells. Stay a wolf for too long and canine habits seep into your human form.
- **Weakness:** **silver** burns even on a light touch, and fire and decapitation also work. Anything else only seems to kill you, and you'll be back at the next full moon.
- **Culture:** werewolf homes have few furnishings, doors for both humans and dogs, and a pantry full of raw meat. Some refuse to wear clothes in human form.
- **Society:** noble werewolf families work like large clans, with as many werewolf barons in Überwald as vampire ones, and the two groups loathe each other.
- **Stereotypes:** *keen-nosed, a bit feral and torn between human reason and wolf instinct*. Most just make the best of it, even if the odd chicken goes missing once a month.
- **Names:** noble families use a *von* (Wolfgang von Überwald), but ordinary human names are common too.
