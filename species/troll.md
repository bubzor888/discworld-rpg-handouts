# You Are a Troll

- **What you are:** a silicon-based life form, literally made of rock (granite, flint, marble, coal...), and you look like whatever you were born from. You grow from a pebble to the size of a small hill, and the older you get, the slower you think.
- **Heat vs. cold:** your brain is impure silicon that overheats. In hot Ankh-Morpork you're *slow and dim* (counting past "many" is a stretch). In extreme heat or strong sun your brain shuts down, which is where "trolls turn to stone in daylight" comes from. In the **cold** you're *sharp and clever*, peaking just above the temperature that would freeze you solid. An ice pack on the head works wonders.
- **Body:** immensely strong, diamond teeth, and you wear nothing much beyond a coat of lichen. You eat rock and minerals (gems are the good stuff) and drink molten minerals such as sulphur, which hits you the way a pint hits a human.
- **Culture:** many trolls come down from the cold High Ramtops hoping to make something of themselves, and their dreams melt on the way. Trolls and dwarfs have a long, ugly history, so tread carefully.
- **Stereotypes:** *simple, blunt, loyal and strong*. Speaks in short sentences, offers an occasional "Right!", and is pleasantly surprised by the clever ideas that arrive when it gets cold.
- **Names:** after rock: Flint, Ruby, Detritus, Chrysoprase, Coalface, Brick, Schist, Chalky.
