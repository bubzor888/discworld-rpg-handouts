# You Are a Human

- **What you are:** the most common species on the Disc. If there's land, humans have made a mess of it. In fact, there's a 99% chance that the person reading this handout right now is a human.
- **Strengths:** adaptability and a talent for survival. You're not stronger than a troll or as sturdy as a dwarf, but are you more clever than a vampire? Also no, but at least you're still alive and kickin', and that has to be worth something.
- **Morporkians:** practical, cynical, assume everyone's out for themselves, and are right about half the time. They love a bargain, a complaint, and a pie in the street, even when they're not sure what it's made of.
- **From elsewhere:** humans live all over the Disc. **Überwald** is fictional Transylvania (nobles have a *von* in their name), **Klatch** is a vast desert region (think the historic Ottoman or Arabic empires), **Ephebe** is a coastal city-state like ancient Greece (philosophers and such), and **Quirm** is basically France.
- **Stereotypes:** See "From elsewhere".
- **Names:** plain (Sam, Fred, Rosie, Trevor), posh (Havelock, Sybil, Mustrum) or evocative (Cut-Me-Own-Throat Dibbler).
