# You Are a Vampire

- **What you are:** an intelligent undead being, most famously from Überwald (think fictional Transylvania). You might be **born** a vampire into a vampire family, or **made** by a bite, though vampires generally prefer not to. You're pale, skinny, very fast and powerful, and can float or turn into bats and other animals.
- **Weaknesses:** the traditional ones: garlic, holy water, holy symbols, stakes, decapitation, fire, and direct sunlight, which turns you to ash (heavy clothes and a broad-brimmed hat help). A drop of blood brings even a pile of ash back.
- **Blood:** you have a "deficit of hemogoblin", but your craving is more of an addiction than a diet. Blood doesn't need to be fresh, or even human.
- **Temperance:** many modern vampires join the **Überwald League of Temperance** (the "Black Ribboners"), giving up human blood and swapping the craving for coffee, photography or politics.
- **Stereotypes:** *cool, poised and stylish*, especially if natural-born (bitten vampires have to work at it). Old-school ones are meticulous and resist modern ideas, while the young rebel with ratty jumpers and ordinary names.
- **Names:** grand names that grow over the centuries as titles pile up, often with a *von* (Sally von Humpeding, Count de Magpyr, Lady Margolotta), or plain and modern (John, Melinda).
