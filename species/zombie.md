# You Are a Zombie

- **What you are:** a corpse that got up again, but **not the brain-eating kind**. You keep your personality, memories and manners. Only humans become zombies (nobody's quite sure why; possibly quantum).
- **How:** a spell, a disruption in the life force, or sheer refusal to accept you're dead. The famous example is **Mr Slant**, the head of the Guild of Lawyers, who refused to stay dead until he was paid.
- **Body:** you control every muscle consciously, and that's tiring. If you're not careful, knocking politely takes the door off its hinges. You don't need to eat or breathe, though bits may fall off now and then. Don't forget your needle and thread.
- **Say:** instead of "braaains", you moan things like *"Why is everybody looking at me funny?"* and *"Could you hand me my hand?"*
- **Stereotypes:** *slow, careful and matter-of-fact about being dead*. Deliberates every move, and treats death as just another inconvenience.
- **Names:** you keep the name you had in life. Some things you can take with you.
