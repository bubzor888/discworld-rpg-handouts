# You Are a Gargoyle

- **What you are:** an urban species of troll, native to Ankh-Morpork's rooftops. You've evolved a partnership with gutters: rainwater runs in your ears and out through sieve-like teeth, which keeps a traditional gargoyle's **mouth permanently open**.
- **Speech:** traditional gargoyles can only make the sounds an open mouth allows, so people must listen closely and fill in the gaps. Great for a one-scene NPC, but a pain in the jaw all session. Luckily, the newest generation of "new age" gargoyles have mastered the valuable talent of **closing their mouths**, allowing proper communication with humans.
- **Movement:** you shuffle in short, jerky bursts like bad stop-motion, and then sit absolutely still for *hours* watching the street.
- **Role:** you see everything from above, so you make an excellent source of information, especially if there are plenty of pigeons about. Pigeons are your cover and your snacks.
- **Indoors:** you are not comfortable coming indoors, and will rarely descend lower than the first floor of a building.
- **Stereotypes:** *patient, observant, a little deadpan*. Has "seen it all", but will only share it if asked nicely or bribed with a pigeon.
- **Names:** after your roost (Cornice-overlooking-Broadway) or your job (Constable Downspout).
