# You Are an Igor

- **What you are:** a hunchbacked, stitched-together and cheerful servant from Überwald (think fictional Transylvania). Anyone important in Überwald has an Igor. Igors typically serve vampires, werewolves and everyone in between. A good Igor is very discreet, never gossips about the bad or just weird things his master has done, and serves loyally (at least until the villagers get the torches).
- **Skills:** you're an **expert stitcher** with an unrivalled knowledge of anatomy, and you're big on self-improvement. When an Igor says he's got his mother's eyes, that's *not* a metaphor. When someone calls for an Igor, you turn up silently at their shoulder, as if you'd been standing there all along.
- **Speech:** a lisp: "Yeth, marthter." Traditional Igors lisp constantly, while *modern* Igors lisp only occasionally, which is enough to be called radical.
- **Ankh-Morpork:** younger Igors come to the city to serve a new kind of master (the rich and powerful, or their art) and work in the hospitals and surgeries.
- **Stereotypes:** *eager, unflappable and practical about gore*. Cheerfully offers a first-aid kit, a spare hand, and some questionable advice, but never a fuss.
- **Names:** Igor. Or Igor. Or, if you're feeling bold, Igorina.
