# You Are a Dwarf

- **What you are:** short, sturdy, bearded. Yes, **all** dwarfs have beards, women included, and traditionally you can't tell the difference (nor are you meant to). You wear leather, chainmail, a woolly vest, and **always carry an axe**.
- **Gender:** traditional dwarfs say "he" for everybody; gender matters less than metallurgy. There are no female pronouns in Dwarvish. Newer "modern" dwarfs (skirts, makeup) are starting to present how they like. Traditional dwarves still disapprove, but in Ankh-Morpork you're protected by city law.
- **Culture:** serious, dutiful, literate and honest. Mining and metalwork are your passions, and you'll sing about gold in a tavern, though you prefer iron for real work. Your axe is a pickaxe on one side and a war axe on the other. Rat is your main meat (rat pie, rat on a stick), usually smothered in sauce to hide the taste.
- **Dwarf Bread:** rock-hard, never goes stale, and occasionally used as a weapon. Proper dwarf bread is not just baked, it's forged.
- **Politics:** trolls and dwarfs have hated each other for ages. In the city it's a rocky truce.
- **Stereotypes:** *gruff, dutiful and pedantic*. Takes statements literally; for example, if told to "pull it together", they might look around to see what is missing.
- **Names:** Gimlet, Cheery, Bashful, Glod, Rocksmacker, Ironfoundersson, Littlebottom.
